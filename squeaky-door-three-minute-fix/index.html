The squeaky door you can fix yourself, and the one you should not

A squeaking hinge is a three-minute job with the right lubricant. A door that sticks, drops or will not latch is telling you something else.

We get a lot of calls about doors, and honestly some of them do not need us. A hinge that squeaks just needs the pin lifted out, wiped and lightly greased. Skip the cooking oil: it collects dust and squeaks again within a month.

Do it yourself

  • Squeaky hinge: lift the pin, clean it, use white lithium grease
  • Loose handle: tighten the set screw under the rose
  • Sticky latch: a dry graphite spray in the latch hole

Call someone

If the door rubs the frame at the top corner, the hinges have pulled loose from the frame or the house has moved. If the key turns but the bolt does not, the lock body is worn inside. Forcing either one usually makes the repair bigger.

Rule of thumb: if it makes a noise, try it yourself. If it stopped moving properly, book it.
