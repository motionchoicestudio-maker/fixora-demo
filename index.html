Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

How is the price calculated?

The technician quotes the whole job at your door, before anything is opened up. Callout and diagnosis are free, repairs start at $39, and the number you agree to is the number you pay.

How fast can a technician arrive?

Most jobs are done the same day, seven days a week, between 7:00 and 23:00. You get an arrival window, plus the name and photo of the person coming, before they set off.

What does the 90-day guarantee cover?

The workmanship on every repair we complete. If the same fault comes back within 90 days, we return and put it right at no extra charge.

Which areas do you serve?

Brooklyn and Queens, and lower Manhattan. If you are just outside that, call us and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you today.

How do I pay?

Card or cash once the work is finished, never before. The receipt reaches your phone with the price, the parts used and the guarantee dates.
